# Data provenance and season contract

The immutable input is `data/raw/APY.csv`; its SHA-256 and accepted/quarantined counts are recorded by `python -m farm_yield.acquisition.dataset` in `data/processed/dataset_audit.json`.

The project owner identifies the [India Data Portal APY dataset](https://ckandev.indiadataportal.com/dataset/area-production-yield-apy) as the upstream source and says this repository uses an already-cleaned Kaggle derivative [owner-provided 2026-11-01]. The exact Kaggle listing, version, cleaning steps and row-level correspondence remain unverified. The supplied portal URL returned HTTP 403 [measured 2026-11-01: `playwright-cli tab-new https://ckandev.indiadataportal.com/dataset/area-production-yield-apy`]; its contents were not inspected. The [OpenDataBay listing](https://www.opendatabay.com/data/government/cb9cdf12-ada1-4f69-9eb2-99a51f91c43b) is a separate possible mirror, not a verified chain of custody. The preserved local file hash identifies the actual training input.

`Crop_Year` is interpreted as the agricultural starting year, based on measured independent comparisons on 2026-10-22. Running `pd.read_csv('data/raw/APY.csv')`, stripping header/season whitespace and summing Wheat/Rabi for 2016 gives Punjab 17,636,000 tonnes over 3,495,000 hectares; Uttar Pradesh 34,971,381 tonnes over 9,884,913 hectares; Rajasthan 12,430,923 tonnes over 3,349,149 hectares. These match the rounded 2016–17 wheat figures in the Ministry of Statistics' [CES 2016–17 report](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/CES_2016_17_20jan21.pdf), PDF pages 114 and 130. The production/area ratios also confirm tonnes/hectare for these labels. This is evidence for the year interpretation; it does not authenticate every district record.

The [Indian Economic Service season definition](https://www.ies.gov.in/arthapedia/concept/cropping-seasons-india-kharif-rabi) identifies the agricultural year as July–June and Rabi as October–March. Accordingly Rabi labelled 2015 uses October 2015 through March 2016 and is evaluated under harvest year 2016. Whole Year uses July 2015–June 2016 reporting observations. Kharif uses June–November of its label year, an intentionally broad fixed window. These reporting windows are proxies: planting and harvest dates by district are unobserved. Other crop/season combinations are quarantined, not silently relabelled.

Sugarcane unit check: [Lok Sabha question 2256, Annexure III](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/15/AU2256.pdf?source=pqals) reports Punjab cane production of 6,607,000 tonnes for 2015–16 and 7,152,000 for 2016–17. Summing APY Whole Year Punjab production for label years 2015/2016 matches both exactly [measured 2026-10-22: raw APY groupby State/Crop_Year]. Punjab is retained; a different statistical table's gur convention does not apply to these APY rows. Chhattisgarh APY totals and implied yields remain inconsistent with the checked cane source and are quarantined without conversion. Other record-level source errors remain possible; matching a state total does not authenticate every district.

Only rows whose supplied yield agrees with production/area within 0.015 tonnes/hectare are accepted, allowing the CSV's two-decimal rounding. Conflicting duplicate keys are quarantined in full. Exact duplicate rows are collapsed once. Geographic joins use normalized state and district names, a unique contemporary district polygon, and a checked weather point inside that polygon. Unresolved names, boundary changes and misplaced points remain explicit exclusions.

Boundaries come from the geoBoundaries API. `data/geo/source_manifest.json` preserves download URLs, source/license information and the pinned geometry revision. Contemporary boundaries do not reconstruct historical boundaries. NASA caches are unchanged; every accepted weather season requires all calendar days and finite values, including previous-year Rabi months.

Satellite extraction uses the same 1 km point buffer during acquisition and serving. It is a district reference-point proxy, without a crop mask; neither the input buffer nor a user-drawn outline establishes field-level yield accuracy. Source native scales are MOD13Q1 NDVI/EVI 250 m (0.0001; SummaryQA 0–1), MOD11A2 daytime LST 1 km (0.02 K then −273.15; good mandatory QA and estimated error ≤1 K), and Sentinel-2 harmonized TOA B8A/B5 NDRE 20 m, masked with Cloud Score+ `cs_cdf ≥ 0.60`. Red-edge band scale cancels in the ratio. Monthly composites use images whose start timestamps fall in the month, with equal image weight; 8/16-day composites can straddle month boundaries. Reduction uses EPSG:4326 at the declared metre scale. Valid pixels and mean per-pixel observation counts are recorded per month; an empty masked result is null, never zero vegetation. TOA atmospheric comparability, no crop mask, different sensors and temporal compositing are experimental limitations.

Vendor definitions, read 2026-10-22: [MOD13Q1](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MOD13Q1), [MOD11A2](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MOD11A2), [Sentinel-2 harmonized](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_HARMONIZED), [Cloud Score+](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_CLOUD_SCORE_PLUS_V1_S2_HARMONIZED). Actual sample verification and acquisition accounting live beside the downloaded observations; documentation alone is not a passed data gate.
